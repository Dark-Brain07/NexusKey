#### NexusKey

**Title:** NexusKey: Bonded Rental Authority Registry
**Description:**
NexusKey is a bonded verification registry for rental authority — not ownership, not a marketplace. Claimants stake a GEN bond behind their right to advertise a property. GenLayer validators assess public evidence, and deterministic contract logic (using the GenLayer AI / Equivalence Principle) settles the outcome. Anyone can file a bonded challenge against an authority claim, using AI consensus to evaluate contradictory evidence.
